01 / GROUNDED IN MY WORK EXPERIENCE

Individuals got faster. The organization did not.

My central observation from work was this:employees were already using many AI tools to become more productive, yet the organization as a whole was not seeing the same improvement.

There was no shortage of ways to use AI. People used office assistants for daily work, workflows for repetitive tasks, and development tools to build their own Agents. An analysis, a prototype or a piece of code could be produced faster, but those gains did not naturally add up to better organizational performance.

WITHIN A ROLE

People completed their own work faster

New tools helped with research, content creation, design, development and repetitive tasks.

BETWEEN ROLES

People still had to reconnect the work

Context had to be explained again, outputs repackaged and standards realigned. Similar capabilities were built repeatedly.

This shifted my focus from getting more people to use AI towardmaking one person’s productivity gains useful to the next person, and retaining that value over time. Organizational efficiency requires addressing information loss in handoffs, duplicate effort and the transfer of experience together.

THE FIRST QUESTION FOR BASE

How can scattered personal AI capabilities become a shared, maintainable work system that supports collaboration across roles?This question led to the template, role customization and project space designs.

02 / CORE VALUE

Four values, centered on organizational efficiency

01 / PRIMARY VALUE

Organizational efficiency: turn each output into a useful input for the next role

Bring role context, working methods, deliverables and task status into a shared workspace. Different roles can work toward the same business goal: one stage’s output feeds the next, proven methods can be reused, and teams spend less time rebuilding, realigning or reworking tasks because information was missing.

The intended shift:from individuals completing their own parts faster to a team delivering the whole outcome more smoothly.

02 / BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Role continuity: keep work moving when people change

When someone new takes over a role, the previous employee’s materials, practical judgment and working habits are often incomplete or missing. The newcomer has to rediscover the process, and output quality can vary with individual experience.

BASE aims to organize that accumulated knowledge into a role Agent: business context, past cases, templates, tools, workflows and criteria for good work. New employees inherit a working foundation they can continue using and improving.

DURING ONBOARDING

Find role-specific materials faster, understand the methods and complete tasks with existing examples.

OVER THE LONG TERM

Work with historical experience and updated rules to reduce repeated trial and error, improve efficiency and keep output more consistent.

The goal is to retain maintainable methods, evidence and experience, rather than every judgment a person makes.Owners still need to update the rules as the business changes.

03 / ENTERPRISE OVERSIGHT

Make AI investment visible, manageable and assessable

An organization needs to know which Agents support which business activities, who maintains them, who can use them, what resources they consume and how well they work. Managing ownership, permissions, versions and runtime information together helps determine which capabilities deserve further investment and wider adoption.

The intended shift:from scattered personal accounts and configurations to organizational assets with clear owners and boundaries.

04 / INDIVIDUAL PRODUCTIVITY

Start with a working capability, then adapt it to the role

Employees can use a suitable Agent template directly or add business materials and adjust how it works. They can complete everyday analysis, writing, research, design and repetitive tasks faster without everyone building from scratch.

The intended shift:lower the barriers to using and building Agents so existing capabilities become part of everyday work.

The four values reinforce one another:ease of use encourages continued adoption; retained expertise supports continuity; management gives reuse clear boundaries; and connected roles create the opportunity for organizational gains.

03 / FROM VALUE TO PRODUCT

Why a role-based Agent workspace?

Following these needs, my thinking evolved from sharing individual Skills to reusable role templates, then to collaboration between roles within a project.

01

Share the method, then add the business context it needs

Sharing a Skill reduces duplicate effort, but a method also needs domain knowledge, past examples, output standards and tools to stay useful in a real role. I therefore expanded the unit of reuse from a single method to a working foundation for that role.

02

Use templates for common ground and customization for role differences

Two teams may both need a product manager Agent but require different inputs, decision criteria and outputs. Sharing one person’s complete Agent may not work for another role. A better approach is toshare reusable templates that people can adapt to their business context.

03

Customize the way work happens, then connect roles through projects

Some tasks need repeated clarification; others need scheduled collection and review, a canvas or a coding workspace. Customization must therefore cover tools, execution and interaction as well as context. Connecting these roles around shared project materials and delivery requirements is the next step toward organizational efficiency.

04 / LESSONS FROM COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

Tools supply capabilities. BASE organizes how roles use them.

WorkBuddy, Qwen Work, Doubao Work and Dify show the development of expert roles, reusable skills, enterprise context, automation and human review. BASE focuses on organizing those capabilities around enterprise roles into a way of working that people can use, maintain and share.

REFERENCEWHAT TO LEARN FROM ITIMPLICATION FOR BASE
WorkBuddyExperts and expert teams make specialized roles and collaboration available as callable capabilities.Use templates to simplify onboarding while allowing roles to adapt materials, methods and delivery requirements.
Qwen WorkAn office ecosystem with skills, connectors, scheduled tasks and enterprise management entry points.Connect everyday tasks and automated runs within one system for both use and management.
Doubao WorkEnterprise knowledge and user permissions support outputs that can return to a collaboration platform.Role Agents should use existing organizational context and contribute new outputs back to it.
DifyWorkflow orchestration and human input nodes incorporate review and branching into execution.Workflows can handle defined steps; business users still need an interface suited to their role and task.

My focus is the practical value of the combination:can employees use it easily, can role capabilities be maintained, and can outputs serve the next person in the process? Existing platforms can supply suitable capabilities while BASE focuses on role configuration, asset relationships and collaboration.

05 / THREE DESIGN HIGHLIGHTS

Reusable. Customizable. Ready to work together.

The prototype explores three priorities: reusing capabilities, tailoring how work happens and collaborating across roles in shared projects. Open the Demo for the full interaction flow.

HIGHLIGHT 01 / TEMPLATES AND CUSTOMIZATION

One person builds a template. Others start with that foundation.

Builders capture professional methods, tools and default workspaces in Agent templates. After finding a suitable template, employees can use its existing configuration or create a role-specific version with their own materials, report formats and capability components.

USE DIRECTLY

Choose a suitable template → Connect required materials and permissions → Start working.

CUSTOMIZE A TEMPLATE

Keep the shared structure → Adjust the role configuration → Preview, try and save an instance.

Design focus:separate using from building, so employees can start working before learning every underlying setting.

HIGHLIGHT 02 / CONFIGURABLE WAYS OF WORKING

Customize context and how the Agent works

Context Engineering gives an Agent the right business grounding. DeepSeek Harness’s component-based approach also offers a way to configure models, tools and execution, with the workspace providing the appropriate interaction. Role differences can then translate into differences in how the Agent actually runs.

CUSTOMIZATION AREAWHAT USERS OR BUILDERS CAN ADAPT
Context and role standardsBusiness materials, rules, past examples, Skills, output structure and acceptance criteria.
Tools and data accessChoose retrieval, file processing, data collection or business connectors, with defined access boundaries.
Models and executionSelect models and capabilities for the task; add delegation and checking steps within a task when needed.
Triggers and interactionManual, scheduled or event-based triggers; chat, tables, canvases and review queues suited to the task.
History and feedbackDecide which task records to retain, which past outputs to reference and how to capture revisions and reasons for rejection.

Design focus:configuration produces a draft that can be previewed, tried and saved. Changes to shared rules, tool permissions or new code require confirmation from the relevant owner.

HIGHLIGHT 03 / PROJECT SPACES

Keep work moving across roles with shared project assets

A project space brings together goals, tasks, materials, deliverables and human checkpoints. People can enter their own role workspace and still understand what the previous stage produced, who acts next and what the following stage needs.

For example, requirements and acceptance criteria from Requirements Insight can feed Prototype Design; the design and its constraints can then flow into development tasks. This requires agreed inputs and outputs, versioned deliverables and named reviewers, beyond simply placing several Agents on a diagram.

Design focus:as work moves between roles, its supporting evidence and decisions stay with it, so project members can build on what already exists.

07 / QUESTIONS TO DEVELOP FURTHER

From useful role tools to lasting organizational value

Who keeps role assets useful over time?

Users provide feedback, domain builders maintain methods and templates, business owners confirm standards, and administrators manage permissions and operations. A role handover should transfer materials, configuration and maintenance responsibility together.

When should a template split or be upgraded?

Differences in materials, output formats and components should first be handled through configuration. Persistent conflicts in goals, acceptance criteria or permissions may justify separate templates. Upgrades should explain what changed and preserve the usability of existing instances.

Where does human judgment remain necessary?

Repetitive work with clear standards suits automation. Goal tradeoffs, key acceptance decisions and actions with external effects retain human confirmation. Reviews should highlight changes and exceptions so they do not become a new bottleneck.

How will we know the organization benefits?

Start by tracking handoffs, rework and duplicated effort along a real business process, alongside onboarding and sustained use. Account for review, configuration and maintenance effort when measuring the time individuals save.

VALUEWHAT TO OBSERVE NEXT
Organizational efficiencyWhether end-to-end delivery time, information-related rework, duplicate builds and repeated alignment decrease.
Role continuityTime for new employees to work independently, delivery quality after handover, and revisions and rework during sustained use.
Enterprise oversightWhether assets have maintainers, permissions and versions are clear, and investment can be linked to usage and outcomes.
Individual productivityNet effort to complete a task, including use, review, customization and maintenance, rather than generation speed alone.

Product development is the current demonstration and trial entry point. Real business needs should determine expansion: make each role’s tools useful and its experience durable, then connect proven capabilities to more organizational processes.
